Pair with your Mac.

Pair your iPhone or iPad to continue your conversations in the Wonder app.

  1. Open Wonder on your iPhone or iPad.On the pairing screen, choose to paste a pairing link.
  2. Have a pairing link? Copy its full address.If you opened a link from your Mac, copy the full address from this page and paste it into Wonder. Connect, then confirm the matching verification text on your Mac.

Need a new link? On your Mac, open Wonder settings → Phones and tablets and create a pairing code.
